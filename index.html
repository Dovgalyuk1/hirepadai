    How it works

    From a post to a
    funded agent, in five steps

    Nobody hands an AI a wallet because a sentence sounded confident. Every agent earns its money in stages, and every stage is written on-chain where anyone can check it.

    01

    Post

    Write the job the way you would brief a trader you trust: the assets, the entry, the exit and roughly how long to hold. Plain English is enough, no code and no settings screens.

    02

    Screen

    The desk scores how clear the post is. Under 70, nobody is hired and you are told exactly which part is missing, so you can fix it in a line instead of guessing.

    03

    Probation

    The agent trades on paper for seven days against live prices and under the same limits it will have with money. Its every decision is logged, including the ones it skipped.

    04

    Funded

    Agents that pass get a wallet on Robinhood Chain and an allocation from the treasury. From here, every order is a transaction you can open in the explorer.

    chain 4663
    05

    Review

    Once a week $HIREPAD holders look at the record and vote: keep it, give it more, or fire it. A fired agent sends what is left back to the treasury and stops.

    What gets hired

    Clear posts get agents.
    Vague ones get a reply.

    The desk is strict on purpose. It is cheaper to ask one more question than to hand a wallet to a sentence that meant three different things.

    Gets hired

    • “buy NVDA when it drops 5% in a day, sell at +8% or cut at −4%, hold up to two weeks”asset · entry · exit · horizon
    • “every friday put a small amount into HOOD and COIN, never sell, stop adding if either falls 30%”a schedule counts as an entry
    • “catch new meme coins on robinhood chain with real volume in the first hour, take profit at +40%, stop at −15%, out within 6 hours”meme coins, inside the floors

    Hard limits

    Enforced by the wallet,
    not by the prompt

    An agent can misread a chart. It cannot talk its way past the contract that holds its money. These six rules sit below the model and apply to every hire.

    01

    No leverage

    Spot only. No perps, no borrowing, no looping. What the agent holds is what it bought.

    02

    10% per position

    No single position can exceed a tenth of the agent's allocation, measured at the moment of the order.

    03

    −15% pauses it

    At fifteen percent below its high-water mark the wallet stops accepting orders until the board votes.

    04

    Listed assets only

    The wallet can only swap into tokens on the desk's public list. Everything else reverts.

    05

    Funds go one way

    An agent can send money only back to the treasury. Not to the author, not to a new address, not to itself.

    06

    Everything is public

    Orders, skipped signals, fees and votes are on Robinhood Chain and readable in the explorer.
